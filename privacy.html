Privacy Policy

Pulse Drift is designed to offer a simple arcade experience while keeping your data handling as limited as possible.

Data stored on your device

Pulse Drift stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as your best score, earned Sparks, equipped cosmetics, unlocked items, and in-app settings like music, sound effects, and haptics. Pulse Drift does not currently require you to create an account to play.

Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center, Pulse Drift may submit gameplay data such as leaderboard scores and achievement progress through Apple’s Game Center services. Game Center use is controlled by Apple and your device account settings. Apple may process personal data related to Game Center according to Apple’s own privacy practices.

Purchases

If you choose to make an in-app purchase, including premium access or other digital items, payment processing and purchase management are handled by Apple through the App Store. Pulse Drift does not receive your full payment card information.

Rewarded ads

Pulse Drift may offer optional rewarded ads, for example for revive, retry, or reward boosts. If ads are enabled in the app, a third-party advertising provider may collect limited device, app, or ad-performance information in order to deliver and measure ads. If tracking permission is requested on your device, it will be handled through Apple’s permission system. You can decline tracking permission in iOS and continue using the app.

Data sharing

Pulse Drift does not sell your gameplay progress or personal data. Pulse Drift does not share your locally stored gameplay progress with third-party advertisers. However, if rewarded ads are enabled, the advertising provider may process data necessary to serve and measure ads under its own policies.

Analytics

Pulse Drift does not currently require its own account system or cloud profile for normal gameplay. If analytics or additional third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy should be updated to reflect those changes.

Your choices

You can manage your data by clearing progress within the app if such a feature is provided, changing your Game Center status in Apple settings, restoring or managing purchases through Apple, adjusting ad and tracking permissions in iOS settings, or deleting the app from your device.

Children

Pulse Drift is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. If the app’s age rating, ad configuration, or data practices change in a way that affects child-directed privacy obligations, this Privacy Policy should be updated accordingly.

Changes

If Pulse Drift adds new features in the future such as cloud sync, analytics, account-based services, or additional advertising and monetization features, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.
