Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Pulse Drift is designed to offer a simple arcade experience while keeping your own gameplay data handling as limited as possible.

Data stored on your device

Pulse Drift stores gameplay-related data locally on your device, such as your best score, earned Sparks, unlocked cosmetics, equipped items, and in-app settings like music, sound effects, and haptics. Pulse Drift does not currently require you to create an account to play.

Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center, Pulse Drift may submit gameplay data such as leaderboard scores and achievement progress through Apple Game Center services. Game Center is controlled by Apple and your device account settings. Apple may process related personal data under Apple’s own privacy practices.

Advertising

Pulse Drift uses advertising features that may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads may be offered for optional gameplay benefits such as revive, retry, or doubled rewards.

Advertising partner data collection

Pulse Drift uses Google AdMob to request, deliver, and measure ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect certain information, which can include device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, diagnostics, performance data, and app interaction data, depending on configuration, device settings, and ad activity. This data may be used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, measurement, diagnostics, and related advertising operations.

Tracking and permissions

If Pulse Drift requests permission for tracking on your device, that request will be shown through Apple’s permission system. You can choose whether or not to allow tracking. Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from using the app, but it may affect the personalization or measurement of ads.

Data sharing

Pulse Drift does not sell your gameplay progress or local save data. Pulse Drift does not run its own account system or cloud backend for normal gameplay. However, third-party services used by the app, such as Apple Game Center or Google AdMob, may process data as needed to provide their services under their own privacy terms and policies.

Analytics

Pulse Drift does not currently use a separate dedicated account-based analytics system for player profiles or cloud accounts. If additional analytics, cloud sync, account systems, or external integrations are added in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

Your choices

You can manage your data by deleting the app, changing your Game Center status in Apple settings, adjusting ad and tracking permissions in iOS settings, and managing your device privacy permissions at any time.

Children

Pulse Drift is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If the app’s age rating, ad setup, or legal obligations change, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect those changes.

Changes

Pulse Drift may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if app features, monetization, data practices, or legal requirements change.
