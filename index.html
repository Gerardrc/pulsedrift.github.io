What Pulse Drift does

  • Offers a fast one-tap endless arcade experience based on timing, precision, and score chasing
  • Lets you collect Sparks and Shards during play
  • Includes unlockable and equipable cosmetics such as skins, trails, and themes
  • Supports leaderboards and achievements through Apple Game Center when available
  • Offers optional premium and rewarded features such as revive, retry, and reward boosts

Troubleshooting

  • If leaderboards or achievements do not appear, make sure Game Center is enabled on your device and that you are signed in.
  • If a purchase does not unlock correctly, use the “Restore purchases” button in the app settings or shop.
  • If rewarded video rewards are unavailable, check your internet connection and try again later.
  • If a cosmetic appears equipped but does not seem visible, start a new run after equipping it from the shop.
